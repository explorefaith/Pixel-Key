Type in six languages. Offline by design.

PixelKey is a free, open-source Android keyboard. Bangla, English, Hindi, Spanish, Chinese and Arabic — with suggestions, autocorrect and voice typing, all processed on your phone.

Privacy

Your words never leave the phone

Typing, prediction and dictionary lookups all run entirely on-device. Passwords, messages and personal dictionaries stay in local storage — there is no cloud to send them to.

Suggestions

Predictions that listen to you

Next-word prediction, tap-to-accept, auto-space after suggestions, and double-space for a full stop. Dismiss the bar per app and it stays dismissed — or flip incognito mode and nothing is learned at all.

Bangla

Avro-style phonetic typing

Type ami and get আমি. Probhat and Jatiyo layouts included, with Bangla word suggestions.

Voice

On-device voice typing

Speech recognition runs locally on your phone. Audio is never recorded or uploaded.

Clipboard

Clipboard that behaves

Recent copies stay one tap away, with pinned items for the things you paste all day. A paste chip pops up right after you copy, sensitive clips are never stored, and unpinned history clears itself after an hour.

Comfort

Made to fit your hands

Adjustable height, one-handed mode, cursor control by swiping on the spacebar, and haptics you can tune.

Speed

Type without lifting a finger

Glide typing traces whole words, backspace swipes delete them, and number rows appear exactly when the field asks for them.

Shortcuts

Text snippets

Save a shortcut once — type /mail, press space, and your full email address appears. Bangla conjuncts and emoji delete as one character, the way backspace always should have worked.

Incognito

A mode for other people's phones

Flip incognito and PixelKey stops learning words and storing clipboard history entirely. Nothing to clean up later, because nothing was kept.

Check the manifest, not the marketing

Privacy claims are easy to make. Here is a fact about the app file itself: typing never touches the network — every keystroke, prediction and dictionary lookup is processed on your phone. The INTERNET permission exists only for the optional in-app update check and is never used while you type.

✓ android.permission.VIBRATE
✓ android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO (voice typing only)
✓ android.permission.INTERNET (update check only — typing never uses it)

Install PixelKey

A direct APK from the releases page — no store, no account, no tracking.

1.
Download the APK

Pick the arm64 build for a modern phone, or universal for anything else.

2.
Allow the install

Android will ask once for permission to install from your browser.

3.
Enable PixelKey

Settings → System → Keyboards → enable, then switch with the keyboard icon.
